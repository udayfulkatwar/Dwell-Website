Services

Can you convert my 2D floor plan to 3D?

Yes — that is our core service. We work from the drawings you provide: PDF, JPG or PNG, plus CAD files (DWG, DXF) for review. Every drawing set is reviewed by our team first, so we can confirm what can be produced and flag any missing information. Conversion is not automatic.

Do you provide interactive walkthroughs?

Yes. Our interactive presentations run in the browser: orbit the exterior, switch to first-person walk mode, move between rooms and jump to preset viewpoints. You can try a full walkthrough now in our live demo, HOUSE H01 — a conceptual three-bedroom house — and our interactive study.

Can your team help with commercial properties?

Yes, we accept commercial inquiries — offices, retail, hospitality and mixed-use projects. Scope and feasibility are confirmed after we review the drawings and requirements.

Can the visualization be used as construction documentation?

No. Our visualizations are illustrative presentations built from the drawings you supply. They are not construction documents, surveys or engineering drawings.

Experience

Can I customize materials and wall colors?

Yes. Within each project we configure a curated set of options — for example wall colors, flooring, exterior cladding, door and window finishes and selected furniture styles — that can be switched in real time. The options are agreed during scoping. On-screen colors are approximate, so confirm final choices with physical samples.

See finishes switch in our study →
Does the experience work on mobile?

Our demonstration runs in modern mobile browsers with touch controls, and it automatically reduces graphics settings on smaller devices. Performance depends on the device, so we agree target devices for each project. Where 3D is not available, a static preview is shown instead.

Can my architect access the visualization online?

Yes. Interactive presentations are browser-based and shared by link, so your architect, builder or family can open them without installing software. Access arrangements are agreed for each project.

Are the lighting previews a solar or daylight study?

No. The morning, afternoon, golden hour and night modes are illustrative lighting previews. A physically accurate solar study needs site orientation, location data and validation, and would be scoped separately.

Files

What files are needed?

Floor plans are the essential starting point. Elevations, sections, key dimensions, material specifications, reference images and furniture references all help us reduce assumptions. You can share PDF, JPG and PNG files, and CAD files (DWG, DXF) for review, through the project form or by email.
