Privacy Policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · version 2026-10-15

This policy explains what personal information ASTRA DWELL collects through this website, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. We collect only what we need to respond to you and to deliver projects.

In short

  • We use what you send us to respond to your request, prepare quotations and deliver projects.
  • We do not sell your information, and we do not add advertising or analytics trackers of our own.
  • Drawings you send us are shared only with authorized team members and are never published.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time: customercare@astragrp.net.

1. Who is responsible for your information

ASTRA DWELL, an architectural visualization studio based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, India, is responsible for the personal information collected through this website. For privacy questions, requests or complaints, contact customercare@astragrp.net. For business correspondence, contact founder@astragrp.net.

2. What we collect

  • Information you give us through our project and consultation forms (Google Forms), by email, or through the assistants — such as your name, email address, phone number, company, country, project details, preferences and messages.
  • Files you send us, such as floor plans, drawings, sketches and reference images.
  • ASTRA DWELL Assistant. The assistant is automated. We do not store full conversations. We store questions it could not answer (shortened, without your IP address) so we can improve its answers, and — only if you choose to share them — the details you send when you ask a person to follow up or request a quotation.
  • Maya. Public pages include Maya, a voice and text assistant provided by ElevenLabs. If you speak or type to Maya, that audio and those messages are sent to ElevenLabs so it can reply. This website does not store that conversation. Please do not share sensitive information with Maya.
  • Security data. To prevent spam and abuse we use your IP address to apply rate limits. We store only a one-way hashed form of it, and delete it within 24 hours. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

Please do not send sensitive personal information (for example health or financial data) through the forms; we do not need it.

3. How we use it, and why we are allowed to

  • To respond to your request and prepare a quotation — based on your consent, which you give when you send us a form or an email, and on steps you ask us to take before any agreement.
  • To deliver and support an agreed project — to perform our agreement with you.
  • To send confirmations and replies about your request — part of responding to you.
  • To keep the website secure and prevent misuse — our legitimate interest in protecting the service and its users.
  • To meet legal obligations, such as keeping business records where the law requires it.

We do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. We do not use your drawings or project details for anything other than your request — including publishing them as portfolio work — without your permission.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that help us run the website and send email: GitHub Pages hosts this website and may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; our email provider delivers messages. They process information on our behalf and only for those purposes.
  • ElevenLabs, when you use Maya. Voice and chat from that widget are processed by ElevenLabs to produce a reply. Their privacy policy is at elevenlabs.io/privacy-policy. Retention of those conversations is controlled in the agent's ElevenLabs settings, not by this website.
  • YouTube (Google), which plays our short studio film in the corner of the page. It is embedded in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com); YouTube receives your IP address and browser details to play it. You can close the player at any time.
  • Google, which hosts our project and consultation forms (Google Forms). When you fill in one of these forms, your answers are stored in ASTRA DWELL's private Google account (Google Forms and Google Sheets) and emailed to our team. Google processes them on our behalf; its privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Your email provider, when you send an email draft this website opens. The page itself does not transmit the form. The message, including any drawings you attach in your email app, is delivered only when you press send, and only to the ASTRA DWELL address shown on the draft.
  • Authorities or advisers when the law requires it, or to protect our rights, our users or the public.

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours. When that happens we take steps to keep it protected to the standard described in this policy.

5. How long we keep it

  • Inquiries, quotation requests, applications, support tickets, assistant follow-up details and uploaded files: as long as needed to respond to you, deliver and support the project, and keep records the law requires. We delete them sooner if you ask and we are not required to keep them.
  • Unanswered assistant questions and requests to speak with a person: deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Hashed security data: deleted within 24 hours.
  • Maya conversations: not stored on this website. ElevenLabs keeps them according to the agent's settings and their privacy policy.
  • Form drafts saved in your browser: stay on your device until you submit the form, clear the draft, or clear your browser storage.

6. How we protect it

Connections to this website are encrypted (HTTPS). Our project and consultation forms are Google Forms: your answers are stored in ASTRA DWELL's private Google account, visible only to our team. Emails you send us are protected by the email providers involved. For confidential drawings, email them to us or share a link restricted to our address rather than one anyone can open. We never publish submissions. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will act promptly if a problem occurs.

7. Cookies and browser storage

  • The public website does not set advertising or analytics cookies of its own. Maya is loaded from ElevenLabs, which may use cookies when you use the assistant, as described in their privacy policy. The corner video is played from YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, which does not set cookies until the video is played; it may then store data in your browser as YouTube describes. Both load only if you choose Allow all when we ask; you can change that at any time. Details are in our Cookie Policy.
  • Some forms can save a draft in your browser's local storage so you don't lose your progress. This stays on your device.
  • The assistant keeps a random conversation identifier in your browser tab's session storage; it is removed when you close the tab.

Links to other websites, such as LinkedIn, are governed by those websites' own privacy policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete;
  • delete your information;
  • restrict or object to how we use it;
  • send your information to you or another provider in a usable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect what we did before you withdrew it).

To make a request, email customercare@astragrp.net — include your project name if you have one. We may need to confirm your identity, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live.

9. Children

This website and our services are intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and its effective date. Our forms record which version of this policy you agreed to.

11. Contact and grievances

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can raise a question or grievance about your personal data with our contact person: the ASTRA DWELL founder, customercare@astragrp.net. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India once it accepts complaints.

Privacy questions, requests and complaints: customercare@astragrp.net
