  1. 01

    Share Your Plans

    Send architectural drawings, floor plans, dimensions and available references.

    • Upload PDF, JPG or PNG drawings with your request. CAD files (DWG, DXF) are accepted for review.
    • Include elevations, sections and material references where you have them — they reduce assumptions.
    • Tell us who the experience is for and how it will be used.
  2. 02

    Scope and Review

    We assess the material, level of detail, supported customizations and deliverables.

    • Every drawing set is reviewed by our team. We confirm what can be produced from it and what is missing.
    • We agree rooms, viewpoints, customization options and lighting presets.
    • You receive a tailored quotation. Work begins only after you approve it.
  3. 03

    3D Development

    Your architectural design is translated into a structured 3D representation.

    • Geometry is built from your drawings, with assumptions recorded and shared for confirmation.
    • Materials, furniture and landscaping are developed to the agreed level of detail.
    • Models are optimized so they perform well in a browser.
  4. 04

    Interactive Experience

    Selected camera views, walkthrough functionality, lighting and customization are prepared.

    • Walkable areas, collision boundaries and viewpoints are configured.
    • Material options and lighting presets are connected to the model.
    • The experience is tested on the target devices agreed during scoping.
  5. 05

    Review and Delivery

    Receive the agreed deliverables, revisions and shareable presentation experience.

    • You review the experience and request changes within the agreed revision rounds.
    • Final deliverables are handed over as specified in your quotation.
    • Questions after delivery go to customer care.

Inputs

What helps us get it right.

Only floor plans are essential. Everything else reduces assumptions.

Floor plans

The essential starting point. PDF, JPG or PNG; CAD (DWG, DXF) accepted for review.

Elevations & sections

Heights, roof forms and facade details — they reduce assumptions considerably.

Dimensions & levels

Key measurements, floor-to-ceiling heights and level changes.

Materials & finishes

Specifications, product references or samples for the surfaces that matter.

Furniture & references

Mood images or specific products for furnished rooms.

Purpose & audience

Who will use the experience — clients, buyers, a planning discussion — and on which devices.

Questions

About the process.

What files are needed?

Floor plans are the essential starting point. Elevations, sections, key dimensions, material specifications, reference images and furniture references all help us reduce assumptions. You can share PDF, JPG and PNG files, and CAD files (DWG, DXF) for review, through the project form or by email.

Schedule a Call →
How can I submit a project?

If you are ready to discuss an engagement, use the Start Your Project application to share your requirements and drawings. If you mainly want to understand cost first, request a quotation instead. An application is reviewed by our team; it is not a confirmed project.

Start your project →Schedule a Call →
What happens after submitting my plans?

Our team reviews your answers, drawings and requirements, may contact you to clarify scope, and then prepares a quotation. Work starts only after you approve the quotation — a submission on its own is not a confirmed project.

See our process →
Can the visualization be used as construction documentation?

No. Our visualizations are illustrative presentations built from the drawings you supply. They are not construction documents, surveys or engineering drawings.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope and the completeness of the drawings. Timelines are agreed in each quotation rather than promised in advance.
